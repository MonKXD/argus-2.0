Ferrowatt Energy — Seed Pitch Deck

Company overview
Ferrowatt Energy builds battery-health monitoring software for commercial solar-plus-storage installations. Founded in 2024, the company is based in Phoenix, Arizona.

Founders
Grace Lin, CEO, previously worked as a battery systems engineer at an energy-storage manufacturer for six years. She holds an MS in Electrical Engineering from Arizona State.
Samuel Osei, CTO, previously worked as a data engineer at a solar-analytics company for four years. He holds a BS in Computer Science from ASU.

Team
The team has 7 full-time employees: 5 engineers, 1 field-operations lead, and Grace.

The problem
Commercial battery storage operators lack early warning of battery degradation, leading to unplanned capacity loss and costly emergency replacements.

The product
Ferrowatt's software analyzes charge-discharge telemetry from battery management systems to predict degradation weeks before it becomes visible in standard state-of-health metrics. The product has been in production for 10 months.

Market position
We are the #1 battery-health monitoring platform in the world, and we have no competitors — nobody else does what we do. Our approach is simply the best in the market, full stop.

Traction
Ferrowatt monitors 40 megawatt-hours of installed battery capacity across 12 commercial sites. Annual recurring revenue is $310,000 as of Q2 2026.

Business model
Ferrowatt charges a per-megawatt-hour-monitored annual subscription fee, currently averaging $7,750 per megawatt-hour per year.

Financials
Ferrowatt has raised $1.2M to date across a pre-seed and bridge round, and has approximately 14 months of runway remaining at current spend.
